About

From the browser to the cloud to your own GPU

As a Senior Software Engineer, I work across the whole stack: responsive front ends, well-designed APIs, and the AWS infrastructure underneath them. I enjoy untangling complex structural problems, applying sound design patterns, and automating workflows so teams can ship faster.

Great software design isn't just clean code. It's building systems that solve real problems and adapt easily to what comes next.

Outside of work, I'm exploring what local LLMs can do when your data never leaves your machine. Solar Forge is where that happens: native apps that keep your data local, AI tools that are inspectable and reversible, and polished additions to the Omarchy Linux desktop.

Solar Forge Diagrams

Offline architecture diagramming for Linux. Draw AWS, Kubernetes, container, and flowchart diagrams on an isometric Three.js canvas, import existing infrastructure, check which traffic can actually flow, and export images, video, or diagram source. No account, server, or internet connection required.

  • TypeScript
  • Three.js
  • Tauri
  • Rust
Open Diagrams Source
  • Official AWS and Kubernetes icon catalogs, plus flowchart shapes
  • Import Terraform, CloudFormation, and Docker Compose, parsed locally
  • Reachability checks from security groups, NACLs, and route tables
  • Animated traffic journeys with saved scenarios and captions
  • Export to PNG, SVG, GIF, MP4, Mermaid, PlantUML, and draw.io
