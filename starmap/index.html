Settings

GitHub token (optional)

Without a token GitHub allows 60 requests an hour from your network, which is about 25 repo fly-ins. A token raises that to 5,000.

Create a fine-grained token with public repositories (read-only) access and no other permissions.

It is saved only in this browser and sent only to api.github.com.

Reading the map

Star
A repository. Colour is its main language, size is its size, brightness is how recently it was pushed, and the halo grows with stars.
Star types
Active star (boiling surface): pushed in the last 30 days. Pulsar (sweeping beams): 5+ stargazers. Ringed giant: 5 MB+. Binary (two orbiting stars): a fork. Remnant (crystal): archived. Asteroid (tumbling rock): a tiny repo.
Constellation
All repos in one language, floating in their own patch of sky. The oldest repo sits at its centre and newer ones form shells around it.
Lines
Each repo linked to its nearest older neighbour in the same constellation.
Planet
A top-level folder. Size is the bytes inside it; colour is its main language. Big folders are gas giants (ringed if they have several sub-folders), tests are ice worlds, CI folders like .github are space stations, and the rest are rocky planets.
Moon
A file, as a little rock coloured by language. Files at the repo root form the belt around the star.
Flares
Commit activity. Scrub the timeline to watch a year of work.
Trade lanes
Imports between folders, scanned on demand when you pick a planet.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan. Double-click a star to fly in; Esc to go back. On the tour: Space or → goes to the next stop, ← goes back, Esc ends it. Pick Manual or an auto-advance time on the tour card.
